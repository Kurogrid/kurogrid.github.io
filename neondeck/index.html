What's inside

Command deck

Greeting, big clock and date. Search with Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Brave, ChatGPT, Perplexity, YouTube or your own engine.

Netlinks

Your sites as cards in collapsible categories. Press / to search and 1–9 to open your top links. Import bookmarks in one click.

Widgets

Weather, world clock, scratchpad, tasks and habits with streaks, Pomodoro, AI quick links. Drag to reorder.

Four themes

Neon Pulse, Red Protocol, Phantom Signal and Acid Orchid.

Your data, your device

Everything stays in local extension storage. Export and import settings as JSON.

English and Turkish

The interface follows your browser language and can be switched in settings.

Alpha status

The core works. Animated backgrounds, CRT and glitch effects, the final icon set and ambient sound are still being built.

Privacy

Installs with storage permission only. Weather sends your chosen city to Open-Meteo only after you agree. Privacy policy · Gizlilik politikası
